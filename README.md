# Instructions

Use the README to 
- Briefly introduce who you are
- Make at least one connection between your background, interests, or prior experience and data science

There is no required length. A few genuine sentences are better than a long paragraph that does not say much. Here are a few questions to get you started if you are not sure what to write:

- What did you do before this program, and how does it connect to data science?
- What drew you to data science specifically?
- What are you hoping to do with a DS degree?
